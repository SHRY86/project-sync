WHAT IS DECENTRALISED?

Each worker owns its beat, score, dance, zone lighting and music commands. BroadcastChannel is same-origin, same-computer communication, not cross-machine networking.

Encore requires all three peer approvals before each local deadline. A partition can produce different approval knowledge: this is not atomic consensus. Missing approvals use the canonical score.

The main thread presents their decisions, mixes audio and provides spectator controls. No continuous conductor.
