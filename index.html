How it works

Each worker owns its oscillator, section, dance, lighting and musical part. Ping/pong estimates relative delay; phase coupling has no reference peer. The display and audio device are shared output adapters.

Encore requires all three approvals before a local deadline. Missing approval means canonical fallback. Asymmetric partitions may produce different local decisions: consensus is not guaranteed.

BroadcastChannel works on the same origin and computer. Private drift and network faults are synthetic.

Keyboard: Space start/pause · M audio · R restart · I isolate LUNA · K kill/rejoin NOVA · E encore · C camera · D guide.
